# TheTanti Catalog Asset Workflow

Use this workflow for saree product images prepared for Meta, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, reels, posters, and later model draping.

## Product Rules

- Every saree photo is a separate product.
- Product IDs use `TT-SAREE-###` in source filename order.
- Do not use `item_group_id` unless the business later confirms real variants.
- Customer-facing copy must not mention AI, generated content, retouching, or editing.
- Price is flat `199.00 INR`.
- Shipping is included. There are no extra charges.
- Keep originals untouched for audit and future model-drape workflows.

## Image Rules

- Remove plastic cover appearance, glare, reflections, wrinkles, packet edges, labels, cards, barcodes, stamps, and packaging stickers.
- Preserve the saree's visible colors, embroidery, print, border, weave, and proportions as much as possible.
- Use a clean warm ivory studio background for catalog images.
- Use square images for Meta catalog.
- Use 1080 x 1920 portrait posters for WhatsApp status and social stories.
- Avoid watermarks, extra text on Meta catalog images, fake labels, people, mannequins, hands, and distorted fabric.

## Saved Prompt

Retouch this product photo so the saree appears clean and professional for ecommerce catalog use. Remove the transparent plastic cover look, including shiny glare streaks, wrinkles, reflections, and crinkled plastic edges. Remove any printed product stamp, label, card, barcode, or packaging sticker visible on the saree. Replace removed label areas with plausible matching saree fabric and embroidery continuation based on surrounding colors and weave. Keep the saree product isolated on a clean warm ivory studio background suitable for Meta catalog and WhatsApp. Preserve the actual colors, border, floral embroidery, woven texture, and visible design. Use realistic product photography retouching with crisp fabric detail. Center the folded saree as a square catalog image with the product fully visible and lightly shadowed. Keep the saree design, colors, and proportions faithful. Do not add people, mannequins, hands, new logos, watermark, or extra text.
